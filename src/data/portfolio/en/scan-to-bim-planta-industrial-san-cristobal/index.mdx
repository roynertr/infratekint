**Client:** Confidential Client

**Collaboration:** Geovértice STL (LiDAR scanning)

**Industry:** Industrial

**Service:** Scan to BIM as-built modeling

**Location:** San Cristóbal, Dominican Republic

## Overview

Between September 2025 and January 2026, INFRATEK developed the full-plant as-built model for an industrial facility in San Cristóbal, Dominican Republic. Geovértice STL performed the LiDAR scanning that enabled the Scan to BIM workflow.

From that capture, we produced high-accuracy models of existing conditions covering architecture, structure, mechanical installations, plumbing, process, and plant equipment, with priority given to process modeling. The scope included **3,392 m² of equipment and process**, DWG, IFC, and RVT deliverables, LOD 200 for the general model and LOD 300 in critical areas, plus integrated asset information.

Together with the client, we also set up a collaborative workflow through a common data environment and an issue-management process to streamline observations, accelerate feedback, and improve information traceability.

## Deliverables

- As-built models for architecture, structure, mechanical installations, plumbing, process, and plant equipment
- **3,392 m²** of equipment and process modeled
- Files in DWG, IFC, and RVT
- LOD 200 development for the general model and LOD 300 in critical areas
- Asset information integration
- Common data environment and issue-management process

## Services Provided

- Scan to BIM as-built modeling
- LiDAR scan interpretation (capture by Geovértice STL)
- Existing conditions modeling
- Multidisciplinary modeling with process prioritization (architecture, structure, mechanical, plumbing, process, and equipment)
- Open BIM / IFC delivery
- Revit modeling and DWG documentation
- Common data environment implementation
- Collaborative issue management

## Result

The client received a reliable digital baseline of existing plant conditions to support future planning, renovations, and technical decision-making.
