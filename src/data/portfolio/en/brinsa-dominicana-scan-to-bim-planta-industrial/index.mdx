**Client:** Brinsa Dominicana

**Collaboration:** Geovértice STL

**Industry:** Industrial

**Service:** Scan to BIM as-built modeling

**Location:** San Gregorio de Nigua, San Cristóbal, Dominican Republic

## Overview

Between September 2025 and January 2026, INFRATEK developed the full-plant as-built model for Brinsa Dominicana in San Gregorio de Nigua, San Cristóbal, Dominican Republic, in collaboration with Geovértice STL.

Through a Scan to BIM approach, we produced high-accuracy models of existing plant conditions covering architecture, structure, mechanical installations, and plant equipment. Deliverables included DWG, IFC, and RVT files, with LOD 200 for the general model and LOD 300 in critical areas, plus integrated asset information.

Together with the client, we also set up a collaborative workflow through a common data environment and an issue-management process to streamline observations, accelerate feedback, and improve information traceability.

## Deliverables

- As-built models for architecture, structure, mechanical installations, and plant equipment
- Files in DWG, IFC, and RVT
- LOD 200 development for the general model and LOD 300 in critical areas
- Asset information integration
- Common data environment and issue-management process

## Services Provided

- Scan to BIM as-built modeling
- Existing conditions modeling
- Multidisciplinary modeling (architecture, structure, mechanical, and equipment)
- Open BIM / IFC delivery
- Revit modeling and DWG documentation
- Common data environment implementation
- Collaborative issue management

## Result

The client received a reliable digital baseline of existing plant conditions to support future planning, renovations, and technical decision-making.
